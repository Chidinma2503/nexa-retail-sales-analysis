# Nexa Retail Stores – Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This is my first Excel data analytics portfolio project. I worked with a retail sales dataset covering January to June 2026 to explore sales performance and understand how different parts of a business contribute to revenue.

I used Microsoft Excel to organise the data, calculate key performance indicators, analyse sales across regions and product categories, and build an interactive dashboard using PivotTables, charts and slicers.

Through this project, I practised turning raw sales records into useful business insights and presenting the results in a clear and interactive format.

## Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large amounts of transaction data, but raw records can make it difficult to monitor performance and identify meaningful patterns.

This project uses Microsoft Excel to transform sales records into an organised analysis that helps explore revenue, profitability and sales trends.

## Project Objectives

- Calculate key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Compare revenue across different regions.
- Analyse revenue contribution by product category.
- Evaluate salesperson performance.
- Examine monthly revenue trends.
- Build an interactive dashboard for exploring sales performance.

## Dataset Description

The practice dataset contains 48 sales transactions and 12 columns, covering:

- Order date and order ID
- Region and salesperson
- Product and category
- Units sold, unit price and unit cost
- Discount percentage
- Revenue and profit

**Note:** The business and dataset are fictional and were created for educational and portfolio practice.

## Tools and Techniques

- Microsoft Excel
- Excel Tables
- PivotTables

- Excel formulas
- Charts and data visualisation
- Interactive slicers
- KPI cards

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The analysis produced the following overall results:

- **Total Revenue:** ₦25,683,750
- **Total Profit:** ₦4,764,550
- **Total Orders:** 48
- **Profit Margin:** 18.55%

## Key Findings

1. **Regional Performance:** Ibadan recorded the highest revenue at ₦9,615,000.
2. **Category Performance:** Electronics contributed approximately 90.5% of total revenue.
3. **Salesperson Performance:** Ada generated the highest revenue at ₦8,104,250.
4. **Monthly Performance:** April recorded the lowest revenue at ₦2,936,500, followed by a recovery in May.

## Dashboard Features

The interactive dashboard includes:

- Four KPI cards.
- Monthly revenue trend chart.
- Revenue by region chart.
- Revenue contribution by category chart.
- Revenue by salesperson chart.
- Region and category slicers for interactive filtering.
- Business insight cards.

## Recommendations

- Investigate the factors behind regional revenue differences.
- Monitor the business's dependence on Electronics revenue.
- Review salesperson performance and identify effective sales practices.
- Investigate the April revenue decline.
- Monitor profitability alongside revenue.
